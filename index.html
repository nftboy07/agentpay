How it works — decision loop & safety limits

Decision loop: task → decompose into purchasable items → collect quotes from allowlisted sellers → negotiate (accept at/below target price; else counter-offer once at the midpoint; accept a counter at/below the cap, else walk away and try the next seller) → policy check (budget remaining, per-payment cap, allowlist, approval threshold) → human approval if above threshold → pay (ERC-20 USDC transfer on Arc) → verify receipt + balance delta → delivery gated on payment → log everything.

Safety limits (all enforced before money moves): total budget (hard stop), per-payment cap (quotes above it are refused outright, no negotiation), seller allowlist, human-approval threshold, kill switch (halts mid-run, state stays inspectable).
